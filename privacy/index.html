Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or browse this website.

Information we collect

We do not run forms on this website. When you call our line, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell the dispatcher, such as your address and the plumbing problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after hearing the recording notice, you agree to that recording.

How we use information

We use call details to understand the problem, dispatch a plumber, give you a price and follow up on the visit. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party content on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may load Google Maps embeds. When those load, your browser connects to Google, which can receive your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising cookies of its own. Third-party content such as maps may set cookies according to the provider's policy.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us what information we hold about your calls or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@proplumbingmarylandheights.com or write to 2331 Millpark Dr, Maryland Heights, MO 63043.

We may update this policy as the site changes, and the current version will always be on this page.
