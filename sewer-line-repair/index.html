Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Maryland Heights, MO

A broken sewer line sends waste back into your house, so the first job is to find exactly where the pipe has failed.

Camera diagnosisSpot repair and lining optionsLateral program help
Call (314) 461-6239

Sewage coming up through a floor drain, a tub or a toilet is an emergency, and a failed sewer line is often the cause. Our dispatch line is answered 24/7, and we book same-day appointments when available. While you wait, stop using water, keep everyone out of the affected room, and turn off power to appliances standing in the water.

A clearing visit gets the house draining again, but it does not fix a cracked pipe. The repair starts with a camera that shows where the line is broken, offset, rooted or collapsed, so the work is cut to the real problem.

From there we lay out the options in plain terms, including the City of Maryland Heights lateral repair program, which can pay for approved repairs.

Emergency steps

Sewer backup: what to do first

In the first minutes

  • Stop all water use, including flushing, laundry and the dishwasher.
  • Keep people and pets away from the backup and wear gloves and boots if you must enter the room.
  • Shut off power at the breaker if water is near outlets, a furnace or a water heater.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. The lowest fixture in the house usually backs up first.
  • Call the 24/7 line. If sewage is in the street or yard near a public manhole, MSD handles the public main at (314) 768-6260.

Signs the problem is the lateral and not a single fixture

Callers describe the same handful of symptoms when a sewer line is failing. Each one tells us something about where to look.

Backups in the lowest drains

A basement floor drain or tub fills first because they sit closest to the line's low point.

Clogs that return after clearing

A cable opens the pipe but leaves roots or a crack, so the problem comes back in days or weeks.

Soggy or sunken yard strip

A wet stripe or small depression along the line can mark a break that is leaking into the soil.

Sewer smell outdoors or in the basement

A cracked pipe or dry trap lets gas escape, and the smell is strongest near the line.

For a quick check on a clog that may not be the lateral at all, our drain cleaning crews can clear a branch line first and camera the main if the water still will not move.

Spot repair, lining or full replacement

Not every damaged sewer line needs a trench across the yard. The right fix depends on how much pipe has failed and where it runs.

OptionFits whenHonest limit
Spot repair by excavationOne short cracked or offset section, reachable from the surfaceDigs up the yard or pavement over the break
Pipe lining (trenchless)A pipe that is intact but cracked, rooted or leaking at jointsCannot fix a collapsed or badly offset pipe, and the pipe must be cleaned first

The camera decides, not the sales pitch. If a spot repair solves it, that is what we recommend, and if the whole run is gone, we say that too.

Local picture

Who owns the sewer lateral in Maryland Heights

The lateral is the homeowner's pipe

The City of Maryland Heights states that the sewer lateral is owned by the homeowner. The lateral is the pipe that carries waste from the house to the public sewer main. The public main in the street is a separate system maintained by MSD, which you can reach at (314) 768-6260.

The city's own page says most lateral repairs cost at least $3,000 and complicated ones can exceed $10,000, and that most homeowner policies do not cover them. Those two facts are why the city program matters, and why you should get the camera findings in hand before you decide anything.

How the city lateral repair program works

Voters approved a $28 annual fee in 1999 for residential properties with six or fewer dwellings, and Prop S in April 2014 raised it to $50. The fee funds the program, because state law bars using general funds for these repairs.

  1. Prove the lateral is the problem

    A camera recording of the cracked, rooted or collapsed section is the clearest evidence you can bring.

  2. Get the Sewer Lateral Claim Form

    The city links the form from its sewer lateral program page, and Public Works can answer eligibility questions at (314) 291-6550.

  3. Understand the coverage

    The city pays 100 percent of an approved repair up to $10,000 and 50 percent of the cost above that.

  4. Handle the permit

    The city's permit page says a permit for any sewer lateral can be pulled without plans, so ask the building division what applies to your job.

More than 700 residents have had repairs through the program since 2000. We cannot promise your claim will be approved, but we can give you the footage and a written scope to attach to it.

Cast iron sewer pipe: what failure looks like

Older homes often have cast iron drain and sewer pipe inside the foundation or just outside it. Over decades, scale builds on the inside, and the bottom of the pipe can rust through while the top still looks solid.

A camera shows flaking and thinning clearly. Short failed sections can be cut out and replaced with PVC using no-hub couplings, while a long corroded run is a case for lining or replacement. We will tell you which one the pipe is asking for.

If you have a septic tank

Most properties in Maryland Heights connect to the public sewer system. If yours has a septic tank, tell the dispatcher on the first call, because a septic problem is diagnosed differently and the tank, not the lateral, may be the source.

Depth and length of the break

A short shallow repair is a different job from a deep one under a driveway.

Method

Spot repair, lining and replacement each use different crews and materials.

Surface restoration

Lawn, concrete and landscaping over the line change the scope.

Access for the camera

Permits and inspection

Where the city requires a permit, it is part of the schedule.

Ask your insurer whether sudden damage from a backup is covered, and expect the answer on the pipe itself to be no. For your actual number, call the line, and read about hydro jetting if the camera shows a pipe that only needs cleaning.

Codes and standards

Standards and rules behind a lateral repair

Sewer pipe is sold to ASTM standards, and PVC sewer pipe made to ASTM D3034 is common for replacement runs. Ask any contractor which pipe standard and which fittings they plan to use, since that detail is part of a fair quote.

The City of Maryland Heights says permits for sewer laterals can be pulled without plans, and its lateral repair program has its own claim form. Get the paperwork sorted before the trench is dug, because the city's reimbursement rules apply to approved repairs only.
