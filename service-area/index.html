Before you call

Who owns the problem depends on the address

A plumbing problem can start on your side of the property line or on the utility's side. Knowing which saves a phone call that goes nowhere.

Water main and meter

Missouri American Water serves the St. Louis County system. If the whole street has low pressure or discolored water, call the utility at 1-866-430-0820 before you pay for a diagnosis.

Gas smell

Leave the building and call Spire at (314) 621-6960 or 911 first. A plumber is the second call, never the first.

Sewer lateral in Maryland Heights

According to the city, the lateral belongs to the property owner. Public Works at (314) 291-6550 explains the lateral repair program for approved repairs.

Public sewer and storm mains

The Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District at (314) 768-6260 handles mains in the street. If a backup affects several houses at once, report it there as well as calling us.

What to have ready when you call

Dispatch works fastest when the details are short and specific. These answers let us say what we can do today.

Four things that speed up dispatch

  • The street address and the nearest cross street, especially in towns outside Maryland Heights.
  • Where the water or sewage is showing up, and whether you can shut the valve off.
  • Whether anyone smells gas or sees water near outlets, appliances or a breaker panel.
  • A callback number in case the call drops while you are in the basement or driving.

Why each town has its own page

A burst pipe in a Chesterfield subdivision and a backed-up sewer in a Hazelwood apartment complex are different jobs. Each town page lists the utility contacts, the likely causes and the first steps for that area, so the advice fits the address.

If you are not sure which town page fits, start with the one nearest to the house and call the 24/7 line to confirm. The services page lists every repair we handle once you know the problem.

Edges of the zone

Some addresses sit close to a ZIP code boundary, and the mailing city on a bill does not always match the town where the house stands. That is common around Earth City, Bridgeton and Creve Coeur, so give us the street and not just the town name.

If your house is outside the towns listed here, call anyway. We will tell you plainly whether a crew can reach you today or whether another plumber is the better call for that address.

A short call costs nothing and saves you a wasted hour of searching while water is on the floor. If we cannot help, we say so right away, so you can move on to the next number.
