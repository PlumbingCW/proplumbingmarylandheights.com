Plumber in Parkwood, Maryland Heights, MO

A practical guide to plumbing repairs, permits and city programs for homes around Parkwood Lane and Parkwood Place.

Call (314) 461-6239

Parkwood is the name used around Parkwood Lane and Parkwood Place in Maryland Heights. The City's parks page lists Parkwood Park at 3145 Parkwood Lane, next to Parkwood Elementary School and near Holy Spirit Catholic Church.

Those landmarks help you confirm you are on the right page. They do not tell you how old a home is or what pipes it has, so this page sticks to decisions that apply to any house here.

Does your plumbing job need a permit?

The City's residential plumbing permit page draws a clear line, and knowing it before you hire anyone keeps a simple repair from stalling.

JobWhat the City says
Clearing a stoppage or repairing a faucetNo permit required for ordinary repair and maintenance
Replacing a water heater up to 120 gallonsPermit available without plans
Repairing or replacing a water line, inside or outsidePermit available without plans
Repairing or replacing a sewer lateralPermit available without plans
Replacing a drain, waste or vent stackPermit available without plans

Permit details can change, so confirm with the building division at (314) 291-6550 before the job. Our water heater installation page covers what installation involves.

Using the sewer lateral program step by step

If a camera inspection finds a broken or blocked lateral, the City program can pay a large share of the repair. The order of steps matters.

  1. Confirm the problem

    A sewer camera inspection shows whether the damage is on your lateral or in the public main.

  2. Check eligibility

    The City program is for residential properties with six or fewer dwellings.

  3. File the claim form

    The Sewer Lateral Claim Form is linked from the City's program page. File it before repairs start.

  4. Schedule the repair

    The City says the program pays 100 percent of repair cost up to $10,000 and 50 percent above that. See our sewer line repair page for how repairs work.

A short list to read before you call

A few details change how a plumber diagnoses the problem.

Gather these first

  • Which fixtures are affected, one drain or several at once
  • When the problem started and whether it comes and goes
  • The water heater label date and tank size
  • Where your main shutoff valve is, and whether it closes fully
  • Any wet spots, stains or a rising water bill

If several drains back up together, the main line is the likely suspect, and drain cleaning or hydro jetting may be the next step.

Which authority handles which pipe

Missouri American Water serves the area's public water, and its customer service line is 1-866-430-0820. MSD, the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District, handles public sewers at (314) 768-6260.

Everything past the connection to the house is yours: the lateral, the water service line and every fixture. When you are unsure where a leak is, a water line repair diagnosis tells you which side of the property line it is on.
