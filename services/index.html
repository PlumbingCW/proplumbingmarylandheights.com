Which plumbing service do you need?

Services marked as 24/7 emergency are the ones where water, sewage or lost hot water cannot wait. For a backup or overflow, start with drain cleaning. For water where it should not be, see burst pipe repair or leak detection.

Planned work such as water heater installation, repiping and basement waterproofing can be booked ahead for a time that suits you. According to the City of Maryland Heights, permits for water heaters up to 120 gallons can be pulled without plans, and the building division can say what else needs one.
