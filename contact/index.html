What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right tools.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening and where, such as a leak under the kitchen sink or sewage in a basement drain.
  • Whether you have shut off the water at the main valve or the fixture.
  • Whether anyone is in danger. If you smell gas, leave and call Spire at (314) 621-6960 or 911 first.

Before you call

If water is spreading, shut off the main valve and the power to wet areas. For a clogged or overflowing drain, stop using water and read our drain cleaning page for what to do while you wait.

For a hot water failure, check the breaker or pilot first, then see water heater repair if it still will not heat.
