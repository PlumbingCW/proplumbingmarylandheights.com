Around-the-clock dispatch

Plumbing problems that cannot wait in Hazelwood

Dispatch runs around the clock, so you do not have to wait for morning to reach a person. These four calls are the ones that most often justify a night visit.

Overflowing toilet

When the bowl keeps rising, shut the valve behind it. If the water is not clean, keep people away and call.

Clogged main drain

Several slow fixtures at once, or a gurgle in the tub when the toilet flushes, point to the main line.

No hot water

A tripped limiter, a failed element or a leaking tank are different problems. Describe what you see to the dispatcher.

Water under a sink

Close the shutoff valves under the cabinet and put a bucket beneath the trap until we arrive.

Most of these start with drain cleaning or toilet repair, and a camera follows if the same fixture backs up again. If the unit is the problem, water heater repair comes first.

Who calls whom

Renters, landlords and the city's registration program

Hazelwood runs a Rental Registration Program through City Hall at 415 Elm Grove Lane, and Code Enforcement handles building codes and permits. If you are a landlord, a registered rental that has an unresolved plumbing complaint is a problem you want to fix quickly. Call us the same day the tenant reports it.

Tenants should report a leak or a backup in writing as well as by phone, and take dated photos. For a property with several units, tell the dispatcher which unit is affected and whether neighbors share the drain, because a blockage upstairs often shows up below.

Decision guide

Water heater: repair or replace?

Many Hazelwood homes and rental units rely on one tank water heater. This table helps you decide before the technician arrives.

SymptomOften meansUsual direction
No hot water, tank is dry on the outsideFailed element, thermostat or igniterRepair

Rust-colored hot water onlyTank interior corroding or anode spentInspect, often replace

A leaking tank cannot be patched, so water heater replacement is the path once the tank wall fails. Ask about the energy and venting options when you choose a new unit.

Step by step

What a sewage backup visit looks like

Backups are the most urgent call we take, so we move in a set order.

  1. Stop adding water

    We confirm every tap, washer and dishwasher in the building is off.

  2. Open the line

    A cable machine at the cleanout or the nearest fixture opens the blockage.

  3. Check the pipe

    A camera shows roots, cracks and sags so we know whether it will return.

  4. Test and advise

    We run water through the lines and tell you whether jetting, repair or monitoring is next.

Prepare

Before we arrive: what to do at a rental or a home

Whether you own or rent, these steps keep the situation from getting worse.

1

Shut off the right valve

Fixture valves sit under sinks and behind toilets. The main valve is where the water line enters the building.

2

Warn the floor below

In a multi-unit building, tell the neighbors underneath so they can move things away from the ceiling.

3

Reach the landlord

Message the property manager now so approval does not slow the repair once the plumber is on site.

4

Photograph and wait

Take a few dated pictures, then keep the area clear for the crew.

Codes and standards

Codes and rules to check in Hazelwood

Hazelwood sets its own permit rules, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) is a model that the city may adopt with its own changes. Ask the city which jobs need a permit, since a like-for-like repair and a replacement are often treated differently.

Water heaters made to the ANSI Z21.10.1 standard need a relief valve and a drain pan where a leak could reach finished floors. Ask the plumber to show you where the discharge pipe ends before the job is closed out.
