Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Maryland Heights, MO

A burst pipe puts hundreds of gallons into a house in an hour, so the first minutes matter more than anything we do afterward.

Burst and cracked pipesCopper, PEX and galvanizedFrozen pipe repairs
Call (314) 461-6239

If a pipe has burst, shut off the main water valve right now. It is usually in the basement, a utility room or near the water meter, and it turns clockwise to close. Then call our 24/7 line, which is answered around the clock, and we book same-day appointments when available in Maryland Heights.

While you wait, switch off power to wet rooms at the breaker, open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and move anything valuable away from the water. Do not walk through standing water near outlets.

A crew will stop the leak, cut out the failed section and replace it with new pipe. When the burst is a sign of failing pipe throughout the house, we explain that and the options before you decide.

Emergency steps

What to do in the first ten minutes

Speed matters, but so does doing these steps in order.

  1. Shut off the main water

    Find the main valve and turn it clockwise. A ball valve closes with a quarter turn of the handle.

  2. Cut the power to wet areas

    If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, turn off the breakers before stepping in.

  3. Drain the system

    Open the lowest faucet and flush toilets once to relieve remaining pressure.

  4. Contain and photograph

    Mop or use towels to slow spread, and take photos of the pipe, the walls and the damage for your insurer.

  5. Call the 24/7 line

    Tell the dispatcher what burst, where and whether the water is off.

If you cannot find the main valve or it will not turn, say so on the call. We have ways to stop the water at the curb or the meter, and the dispatcher will tell you what to do.

Why pipes burst: copper, PEX and galvanized

Every pipe material fails in its own way, which is why identifying the pipe is the first diagnostic step.

MaterialHow it failsTypical repair
CopperPinhole corrosion, freeze splits, failed solder jointsCut out the section and sweat in new copper or use push-fit couplings
PEXFreeze expansion, fitting failures, UV damageReplace the damaged run or fitting with crimped or expansion connections
Galvanized steelInternal rust narrowing the bore, then splittingReplace the section, and consider repiping if rust is widespread
CPVCBrittleness with age, cracked fittingsReplace the section with new CPVC and fittings

If galvanized pipe is bursting in more than one place, spot repairs start to look like a poor use of money, and repiping deserves a closer look.

Frozen pipes that burst

Freezing water expands inside a pipe, and the pipe usually splits at the weakest point. The split often does not leak until the ice melts, which is why a flood can start hours after the cold snap ends.

If you find a frozen pipe that has not yet burst, keep the faucet slightly open and warm the area gently with a hair dryer or space heater placed safely away from anything flammable. If you see a split or hear water running behind a wall, shut off the main and call us. Insulating pipes in unheated spaces helps prevent repeat failures.

Cracked drain pipes and supply valves

Not every burst is on the pressurized side. A cracked drain line can leak into a wall or under a floor when water runs, and a failed supply valve under a sink or behind a toilet can spray water until the main is closed.

A broken fixture valve is a smaller repair, and it is covered in more detail on our faucet repair page. A cracked drain pipe under a slab or in a wall may require a camera and a targeted opening, which is why we sometimes bring in leak detection equipment to find it.

Repair methods and when each fits

The repair depends on how much pipe is damaged and where it sits.

MethodBest forTrade-off
Section replacementA single split or pinhole on a straight runRequires access to cut and join
Coupling or repair clampA temporary or small repairOften a short-term fix on old pipe
Rerouting a runA leak inside an inaccessible wall or slabAdds new pipe in exposed spaces
RepipeRepeated failures across the houseLarger project, but ends the cycle of leaks

We explain the options plainly. If the pipe around the burst is weak, a short repair may not hold, and we say so.

Insurance, documentation and water damage

What to do for your claim

Sudden burst damage is often covered by homeowner policies, while wear and gradual corrosion usually are not. Ask your insurer which category your loss falls into.

Photograph the failed pipe before it is removed, the wet materials and the water line on the walls. Keep the damaged section if the plumber gives it to you. A restoration company handles drying, and we focus on stopping the water and repairing the pipe.

Codes and standards

Pipe standards that decide a repair

Copper tube is made to ASTM B88 and PEX tubing to ASTM F876, and the repair fitting has to match the pipe type. A fitting made for one material can fail on another, so a plumber should name the pipe and the fitting before cutting.

Stopping a leak is ordinary repair under the City of Maryland Heights permit rules. Replacing water lines is not, so ask the building division when a repair turns into a replacement.
