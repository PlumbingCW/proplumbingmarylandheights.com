Prepare

Before we arrive: five minutes that limit the damage

What you do before the truck pulls up decides how much water reaches your floors. Four quick steps help most.

1

Find the main valve

It sits where the water line enters the house, often near the water heater or in a utility closet. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Cut power near water

Switch off breakers for outlets and appliances close to standing water. Never wade into a flooded basement to reach the panel.

3

Clear a path

Move rugs, boxes and furniture away from the leak or the cleanout. Office and hotel managers can send a staff member to meet the plumber at the door.

4

Take photos

A few pictures of the damage and the shutoff help with an insurance claim later. Do not wait on them if water is still running.
